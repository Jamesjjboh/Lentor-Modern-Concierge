# Privacy Policy — Lentor Modern Concierge Bot (@LMConciergeBot)

*Last Updated: October 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Lentor Modern Concierge** Telegram Bot ("the Bot", "we", "our") handles information when you interact with it.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you use the Bot on Telegram, we process minimal operational information necessary to provide the concierge service:
- **Telegram Account Metadata:** Your Telegram User ID, first name, and username (used for sliding-window rate limiting, preventing bot abuse, and session management).
- **Messages & Queries:** The text and images you send to the Bot, processed to answer queries regarding estate facilities, contacts, defect submissions, and guidelines.
- **System Logs:** Technical error logs and performance metrics hosted on Google Cloud Run.

### 2. How Your Information Is Used
- To answer your estate queries accurately using AI assistance.
- To enforce rate limits and ensure service availability for all residents.
- To monitor bot health, fix technical bugs, and improve estate FAQ coverage.

### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **AI Processing:** Queries are processed securely via Google Gemini APIs to generate responses. Your data is not sold or shared with commercial data brokers or advertisers.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** We do not track your location, read your contact lists, or monitor your other Telegram chats.

### 4. Data Retention
- Chat interaction context is retained transiently in session memory to maintain conversational flow.
- Server logs containing Telegram IDs are purged periodically and used solely for operational security and diagnostics.

### 5. Disclaimer & Community Notice
The Lentor Modern Concierge Bot is an independent digital assistant created for the convenience of Lentor Modern residents. While we strive to provide the most updated information from official MCST circulars, guidelines, and manuals, residents should verify official bylaws, bookings, and fee structures directly with the Managing Agent or MCST office.

### 6. Contact & Deletion Requests
If you wish to clear your session or have questions regarding this bot, please contact the administrator via Telegram or open an issue on the project GitHub repository: [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Jamesjjboh/Lentor-Modern-Concierge).
